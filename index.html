Catch-up

by Amplifiers · for Mac

Your WhatsApp groups, caught up for you: one short card per group with what was said, what was decided, and anything waiting on you.

Catch-up for Mac with Apple SiliconM1, M2, M3, M4 and later Catch-up for Mac with IntelOlder Macs, mostly from before 2021

Not sure? Open the Apple menu → About This Mac. If it says Chip: Apple M…, pick Apple Silicon. If it says Processor: Intel, pick Intel.

Installing

  1. Open the downloaded file and drag Catch-up into Applications.
  2. Open Catch-up from Applications and follow the setup: link WhatsApp, add your Anthropic API key, pick your groups.
  3. That’s it. Catch-up updates itself when a new version comes out.
Needs macOS 13 Ventura or later Runs on your Mac. Only reads; never posts in your groups.
